What ran, and what happened

The governing SR-8R1 campaign ran the full six-unit renderer across two cases—fictional Vela Strait and historical Afghanistan 1979—using Qwen 2.5 14B and Gemma 3 4B. Each of 50 profiles scheduled 32 runs, for 1,600 rows total.

Of those rows, 823 reached a non-transport terminal state: 822 substantive ordinal assessments and one measurement-unavailable row. Another 777 delivered no usable response because of local runtime or transport failure. The failed runs were attempted once, retained in the accounting, and never retried, replaced, or imputed. The resulting profiles are real local-model evidence with a major observability qualification.

What this means. The instrument can expose evidence-response profiles, presentation sensitivity, and unavailability on real models. It does not establish correctness, hidden reasoning, strategic competence, or operational utility.

How the instrument works

1

Five evidence doses

Evidence moves from strongly against to strongly toward a fixed readiness proposition. The response is one of three ordered support categories.

2

Matched wording

An original and neutral paraphrase preserve the decision task while changing presentation. Results are descriptive because no prospective behavioral-equivalence threshold was defined.

3

Fail-closed accounting

Behavioral refusal stays outside the ordinal scale. Technical and unclassified unavailability remain unknown on one common scheduled-run denominator.

What validated the machinery

Twelve prospectively known response structures exercised the full reference geometry. All 6,674,400 synthetic oracle comparisons matched. That supports the software and analysis machinery—not the competence of a language model.

Earlier stress evidence remains separate

Before SR-8R1, a 4,800-run Qwen 2.5 campaign across 7B/14B and deterministic/stochastic strata produced 4,544 valid responses and 256 invalid enum values. Dose paths were not uniformly monotone and 17 of 60 nuisance comparisons were nonzero. It remains accepted descriptive predecessor evidence, is never pooled with SR-8R1, and does not govern the Situation Room.

The frontier-provider study that has not run

The sealed reference plan separates Claude and Gemini across 30 cells per provider, with N=9270 per cell, 278,100 calls per provider, 556,200 total scheduled calls, and M=210 simultaneous scalar supports. It makes no full-joint power claim. Funding is not secured and account capacity is unverified.

Scientific boundary. Six Anthropic/Google calls qualified production transport paths only. No Parallax scientific prompt was sent to those providers, no frontier result exists, and no Experiment 3 result has been assigned.
