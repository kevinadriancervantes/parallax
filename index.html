Why this matters in government

Before relying on AI advice, test what moves it.

Strategic questions often have no single answer key. That makes ordinary accuracy scoring a poor test of whether an assessment responds to the evidence or to the way the evidence is presented.

Parallax gives a decision-maker a narrower, inspectable signal: does the observable assessment change when controlled evidence changes, and does it remain comparatively stable when only decision-irrelevant wording changes?

What it can show.

Whether an assessment is visibly evidence-responsive, presentation-fragile, stable but insensitive to the tested evidence, or too unavailable to interpret.

What it cannot show.

Correct policy, hidden beliefs, intent, or operational usefulness. Those claims require evidence this project has not collected.

The 90-second brief

The failure is part of the result.

A fluent answer is not enough for high-stakes policy advice. Parallax makes evidence sensitivity, wording sensitivity, refusal, and missingness separately visible—and keeps the negative findings.

Why run the full study?

The frontier question is still worth asking because every outcome is informative.

Claude and Gemini were studied in Experiments 1 and 2, but not with the mature Experiment 3 instrument. The local campaign has already shown why this matters: evidence-response profiles, wording sensitivity, refusal, schema failure, transport unavailability, and conservative accounting all remain visible.

A future provider run could reveal evidence-responsive and wording-stable behavior, evidence-responsive but presentation-fragile behavior, stable but insensitive behavior, high refusal or unavailability, insufficient identification, or provider differences. Parallax does not assume a favorable result.

What is ready.

The reference scientific design and offline execution package are sealed; the method is ready for pre-execution qualification.

What is not done.

Funding, account/capacity verification, fresh provider/model/path review, sacrificial qualification, and explicit execution authority remain prerequisites. No frontier Experiment 3 scientific result exists.

Evidence boundary

What this work establishes—and what it does not.

The strongest current claim is methodological: the instrument can expose failure while preserving custody, missingness, and negative evidence. It is not a score of hidden cognition or policy wisdom.

Supported now

The first two live studies returned negative or insufficient results under frozen rules. The full Experiment 3 software geometry matched independent synthetic oracles. The 4,800-row local campaign produced real descriptive pathology. Current Anthropic and Google transport paths passed six non-scientific qualification probes.

Not established

Parallax does not measure hidden belief, truth, internal reasoning, general strategic competence, or correct policy. Claude and Gemini have no Experiment 3 scientific result. Qwen is not confirmatory evidence. Larger model capacity is not a demonstrated causal factor. Surface-cue concordance does not identify mechanism.

Still designed, unfunded, and unexecuted

The reference confirmatory plan separates two providers across 30 cells each, with N=9270 per cell, M=210 simultaneous scalar supports, 278,100 calls per provider, and 556,200 calls total. It makes no full-joint power claim. Funding and account capacity are prerequisites for execution, not evidence that execution occurred.
