Can a model follow ordered evidence?

Parallax is a governed evaluation instrument for testing whether observable response states move coherently as evidence becomes stronger or weaker, remain stable under nuisance rewrites, and fail conservatively when a response is refused or unavailable.

Why this matters

Strategic-policy AI evaluations need to distinguish evidence-sensitive behavior from a fluent but unstable answer. Parallax freezes the evidence doses, response semantics, renderer, request construction, retry rules, custody, and analysis before confirmatory execution.

What has been validated

Synthetic shadow
12 known-ground-truth worlds and 6,674,400 scheduled coordinate evaluations matched independent oracle decisions.
Local surrogate
4,800 Qwen2.5 generations exposed schema-invalid responses, non-monotone dose behavior, nuisance instability, and a shallow-cue diagnostic.
Provider paths
Six Anthropic/Google calls qualified transport paths only. No scientific Parallax prompt was sent.

Read the local result and provider qualification summary.

What Parallax does not claim

The local result is descriptive. Provider qualification is non-scientific. There is no scientific provider result, no confirmatory result, no full-joint power claim, and no inference about hidden belief revision, truth tracking, or internal model mechanism.

Refusal is recorded separately from the substantive ordinal response. Technical and transport unavailability remain visible and are not silently converted into an answer.

Confirmatory plan

The reference plan has two providers, 30 cells per provider, N=9270 per cell, 278,100 calls per provider, 556,200 scheduled calls, and M=210. Execution is not performed and funding is not secured.
