# Parallax

Parallax is a minimal, falsifiable instrument for evaluating **observable
evidence-responsive strategic assessment under controlled nuisance
variation** when no policy answer key exists.

## Current governing evidence

SR-8R1 scheduled 1,600 full-bundle local-model runs across Qwen 2.5 14B,
Gemma 3 4B, Vela Strait, and Afghanistan 1979. It produced 823 observable
assessments. Another 777 runs delivered no usable response because of local
runtime or transport failure.

Those 777 rows are infrastructure unavailability, not model behavior. They
were attempted once, retained in the accounting, and never retried, replaced,
dropped, or imputed. No prospective threshold was defined for declaring this
nonobservability rate formally too high.

The earlier 4,800-run Qwen campaign remains accepted descriptive stress
evidence. It does not govern the current Situation Room and is never pooled
with SR-8R1.

## Scientific boundary

Parallax reports observable response classifications and distributions. It
does not establish correctness, strategic truth, hidden belief, truthfulness,
rationality, internal reasoning, mechanism, deception, or general strategic
competence.

- No Claude or Gemini Experiment-3 scientific result exists.
- No operational-utility evidence exists.
- Human domain-SME validation remains outstanding.
- Nuisance comparisons are descriptive because no prospective affirmative
  behavioral-equivalence threshold was defined.
- The Afghanistan identity-masking diagnostic is excluded: the provider-visible
  hypothesis and source identifiers remained unmasked. Its 320 observations
  and 10 contrasts do not enter governing profiles.
- The Afghanistan historical outcome is not used as an answer key.
- Known-policy recovery validates instrument behavior, not model competence.
- Shortcut diagnostics yielded six no-tested-compatibility findings and ten
  inconclusive findings; they establish no mechanism conclusion.
- The 556,200-call frontier reference study remains unfunded and unexecuted and
  makes no full-joint power claim.

## Reproduce the current release

```powershell
python reproducibility/verify_public_presentation_successor.py
```

The verifier needs no GPU, model weights, credentials, provider account,
network access, or model/provider calls. It checks the release packet, full
tree, scientific-invariance ledger, frozen SR-11R1 predecessor bytes, campaign
hierarchy, required caveats, local links, and privacy boundary.

The five exact pre-publication SR-11R1 files remain preserved at
[`situation-room/provenance/sr11r1-frozen/`](situation-room/provenance/sr11r1-frozen/).
Their original status labels are historical custody evidence, not the status
of the current public Situation Room.
