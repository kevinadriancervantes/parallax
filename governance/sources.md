## August 23 ChinaTalk contest context

- [Official $50k ChinaTalk submission/hiring contest](https://www.chinatalk.media/p/50k-chinatalk-submission-hiring-contest)
- [Official Fillout submission form](https://forms.fillout.com/t/sSHe7qxvzrus)

round is one paragraph per idea, with up to three ideas. The inspected public
form did not expose an authoritative deadline clock, timezone, or close
timestamp. See [deadline receipt](../submission-aug23/deadline-receipt.json).

## Qwen2.5 context

- [Official Qwen2.5 announcement](https://qwenlm.github.io/blog/qwen2.5/)

The source identifies the Qwen Team and the open-weight Qwen2.5 family,
including the 7B and 14B sizes used locally. Parallax describes this as an
Alibaba-origin/open Qwen family. It does not treat Qwen as representative of
Chinese models generally or as a proxy for Anthropic/Google results.
