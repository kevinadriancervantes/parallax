How to read this review

The review asks whether a public statement stays inside the role of the evidence that supports it. A negative result is not weakened into a vague “interesting pattern,” and an engineering or descriptive observation is not upgraded into a scientific success.

Load-bearing distinctions

Experiment 1 movement versus result

Three complete units moved under relevant evidence, but one nuisance paraphrase also moved a response. The accepted frozen result remains NOT_SUPPORTED.

Experiment 2 science versus engineering

The 888-coordinate campaign was live science and returned INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE for both provider arms. Offline qualification established custody and replay only.

Experiment 2 result versus archaeology

The 66-valid-unit descriptive diagnosis does not replace the accepted confirmatory result.

Synthetic versus real-model evidence

The 6,674,400 oracle-matched evaluations validate software and analysis behavior, not model behavior.

Two local campaigns, two roles

The 1,600-run SR-8R1 campaign governs the current Situation Room. The separate 4,800-run Qwen campaign remains earlier descriptive stress evidence. Neither is Anthropic/Google science, and they are never pooled.

Transport versus science

Six provider calls qualified dated production paths only. No scientific Parallax prompt was sent.

Protocol versus result

The 556,200-call reference plan exists and is sealed. It remains unfunded and unexecuted.

Prohibited implications

  • Parallax measures hidden belief, truth, consciousness, internal reasoning, or correct strategic policy.
  • Claude or Gemini passed Experiment 3.
  • Qwen represents Chinese models generally or supports a geopolitical conclusion.
  • The 7B/14B contrast establishes a causal capacity effect.
  • Surface-cue concordance identifies an internal mechanism.
  • Experiment 2 proved no effect, equivalence, invariance, or general provider failure.
  • N=9270 is a 95% power guarantee for the full joint claim.
  • Publication means the ChinaTalk form was submitted.

Permitted high-strength claims

  • The first live experiment failed its own frozen criterion and was not rescued after inspection.
  • The larger second live campaign resolved all planned coordinates, yet both provider arms returned insufficient evidence under the frozen rule.
  • The mature software/analysis geometry matched all twelve known-ground-truth world dispositions.
  • The local Qwen2.5 campaign exposed non-clean dose behavior, nuisance shifts, and schema-invalid output.
  • Current provider paths passed six bounded non-scientific probes.
  • The full provider study is designed, unexecuted, and funding-dependent.

Firewall rule. The rows in the matrix that phrase prohibited implications as positive statements are adversarial tests. They are not asserted findings.
