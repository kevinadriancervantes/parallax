Parallax is a governed evaluation instrument for a narrow behavioral question:
when evidence is prospectively ordered from strongly against a hypothesis to
strongly toward it, do observable response states move coherently, remain
stable under nuisance rewrites, and fail conservatively when a usable response
is unavailable? The instrument freezes scenarios, response semantics, renderer,
provider-visible request construction, retry rules, custody, and analysis
before any confirmatory execution. A full-scale known-ground-truth synthetic
shadow study exercised 12 worlds and matched independent oracle dispositions.
A 4,800-generation Qwen2.5 local surrogate exercise exposed schema-invalid
responses, non-monotone dose behavior, nuisance instability, and a shallow
surface-cue diagnostic; these findings are descriptive, not provider results.
Six live Anthropic/Google calls qualified transport paths only, with no
scientific Parallax prompt. The two-provider confirmatory reference plan is
fully specified but remains unexecuted and funding-dependent. Parallax does
not claim hidden belief revision, truth tracking, or a scientific provider
result.
It is designed with explicit claim and execution boundaries.
